# Caption Writing Guidelines

1. **Identify the goal.** Note the platform, audience, post objective, relevant keyword, image or experience, and desired reader action.
2. **Choose one angle.** Focus on a single topic, such as nature, accommodation, romance, family, dining, guest experience, location, or booking.
3. **Write an inviting opening.** Make the first line specific to the post. Avoid generic hooks and exaggerated claims.
4. **Use verified details only.** Describe what is visible or confirmed in project materials. Never invent facilities, services, prices, offers, availability, distances, booking policies, or guest reviews. Leave uncertain details out or mark them `[confirm detail]`.
5. **Connect the scene to a feeling.** Keep the voice calm, warm, nature-led, and focused on thoughtful stays, privacy, and a slower pace. Do not promise unverified outcomes.
6. **Add one clear call to action.** Make it relevant to the post, such as inviting readers to enquire or plan a stay.
7. **Include verified booking details in the CTA.** Current details listed on the official contact page (checked 26 November 2026): Call or WhatsApp **+91 88918 88818**; website: [chembarathi.com](https://www.chembarathi.com); direct booking: [Book your stay](https://bookingengine.stayflexi.com/?hotel_id=28009). Recheck the official site before publishing and copy the relevant detail accurately. Never guess or reuse unverified contact information. If a detail is unavailable or cannot be verified, use `[add verified booking contact]` and confirm it before publishing.
8. **Use keywords naturally.** Include a relevant phrase such as `Chembarathi Wayanad` where it fits. Avoid repetition and awkward SEO wording.
9. **Add a restrained set of relevant hashtags.** Use accurate brand, location, and post-topic hashtags. Avoid unrelated or unverified tags.
10. **Keep the caption easy to scan.** Use short paragraphs, plain language, and emojis sparingly.
11. **Save and review.** Keep `caption.md` caption-only, without a title or heading. Review the caption for accuracy, tone, clarity, working booking details, and unsupported claims before delivery.
